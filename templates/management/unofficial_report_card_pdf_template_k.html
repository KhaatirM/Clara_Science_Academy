Academic Standards: The Clara Science Academy (CSA) reports student performance quarterly. This progress report lists the skills outlined by the NC Department of Education Standards. This framework provides guidance about what a Kindergarten student should know or be able to do. The K12 curriculum is the core set of courses used by CSA to help Kindergarten students meet these standards. Individualized goals are set for each student. The teacher and learning coach determine the percentage of course completion necessary to meet the academic goals for the student based on the standards and anchors. The year-to-date course progress reflects the progress towards meeting standards as well as academic goals related to course completion. The decision to promote a student to the next level is determined by the teacher.
